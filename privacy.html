Privacy Policy for Block Up!

Last updated: August 24, 2026 · Terms of Use

GAMEBUSTER LTD ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile game Block Up! (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you download, install, and play Block Up!.

1. Information We Collect

We and our third-party service providers collect information automatically when you use Block Up!:

Device & Technical Information
Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (e.g., IDFA, Android Advertising ID/GAID), IP address, language settings, and network status.
Gameplay & Usage Data
Game progress, scores, in-game achievements, session duration, crash reports, and interaction with game features.
Advertising Data
Information about ads viewed within the Game, clicks, impressions, and user interactions with advertisements.

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as your real name, physical address, financial card details, or precise GPS location.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected information for the following purposes:

  • Ensuring core Game functionality and performance optimization.
  • Monitoring analytics, crash detection, and bug fixing.
  • Serving contextual and personalized in-game advertisements.
  • Complying with legal and platform obligations (Google Play, Apple App Store).

3. Third-Party Services and SDKs

Block Up! integrates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) for analytics and advertising. These third parties may collect and process information under their respective privacy policies:

  • Analytics Providers: Google Analytics for Firebase, GameAnalytics, Unity Analytics.
  • Advertising Networks: Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, ironSource, Mintegral.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Block Up! is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain EU jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that personal data from a child has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete such data.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR in the EEA/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California), you have the following rights:

  • Access, review, or request a copy of the personal data collected about you.
  • Request the erasure/deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of personalized/targeted advertising via your device settings:
    • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Disable tracking for Block Up!.
    • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID / Reset advertising ID.

To request data deletion, contact us at games@clawbuster.com.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
