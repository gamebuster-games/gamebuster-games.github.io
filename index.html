How to play

  1. Drag & place. Put pieces onto the 8×8 board — and feel the board respond.
  2. Push & slide. Placed blocks shove their neighbours. Use it — push a stray block into a line you couldn't reach.
  3. Crush & score. Fill a full row or column and enjoy the cascade.
  4. Chase combos. Set up several lines at once for the biggest points.
  5. Stay zen. Keep the board tidy, think one push ahead, and let the combos come to you.
